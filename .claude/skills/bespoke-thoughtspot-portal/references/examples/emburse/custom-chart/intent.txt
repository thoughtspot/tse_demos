Animated motion bubble chart "Policy risk in motion" for the Emburse KPIs tab. One bubble per Department; x = Total Expense Amount (spend in the quarter), y = Out-of-Policy %, bubble area = Total Violations; a Play control steps through quarters (axes fixed) so viewers watch Sales climb above a dashed 10% risk threshold in 2026 while other departments stay in the 4-8% band. Partial quarters at either end (very low spend) are dropped. Sales in Emburse primary blue #015CB7, the other departments in muted blue #9BB8D0, navy ink #041E3E. Title computed from the latest frame (e.g. "Sales out-of-policy spend reached 11.8% in Q3 2026, up from 7.0%"). Hover shows department, spend, OOP %, violations; click selects.
