Ecosurety "RAM sorting line": a bespoke packaging-recyclability conveyor. One horizontal lane per packaging material (Plastic, Paper and Card, Glass, Aluminium, Steel, Fibre-based Composite, Wood, Other), ordered by tonnage. Each lane is a conveyor whose belt is split into Green / Amber / Red segments by share of tonnage under RAM for the selected quarter; small parcels (packs) travel along the belt and drop into the coloured bin. Left: material name + tonnage. Right: modulated EPR fee per tonne. Header: title, subtitle with overall Red-rated share for the quarter and its change vs first quarter. Controls: Sector select, Quarter select + Play button that animates quarters (story: Health & Beauty plastic Red share falls 34% -> 11%). Hover any segment: tooltip with tonnes, share, fee. Theme from [Theme Mode]. Font Manrope. Fits 520x460.
