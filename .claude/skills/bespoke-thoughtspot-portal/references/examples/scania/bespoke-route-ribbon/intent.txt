Scania route ribbon (fleet fuel efficiency, connected-services analytics for hauliers).
Metaphor: one road per country; the ribbon is the road for the last 12 months, its width = km driven that month
(sqrt scale), its colour = idle share (or L/100 km via the metric dropdown) on the Scania blue ramp
(#E6EEF9 -> #7FA6DD -> #2058A8 -> #041E42; domain = lane minimum .. hot threshold). Months at or above the hot threshold
(1.6x the median idle share, 1.07x the median L/100 km) turn Scania red #D6001C with a pulsing glow. A truck drives each
road; Play walks the months (future segments fade) and the right-hand value updates. Lanes sorted by the latest month's
value (hottest first), capped at 8. Application dropdown (default Long Haulage). Partial months (< 40% of the median km)
are dropped. Story: Poland long haulage idle share 8.0% -> 23.4% (Oct 2026) vs fleet median 7.7%.
