HMR market-share blister pack. A pharmacy blister card of 100 pills (10 x 10); each pill = 1% of units sold in the chosen ATC market and region for the shown month, coloured by product (originator brand in deep navy-teal, generics in teal shades, OTC in amber). Play animates month by month: in C10 statins the Cardiolis pills flip to generic colours as generic share climbs 40% -> 84% after loss of exclusivity. Controls: market select, region select, play/pause, month scrubber. Hover a pill: product, type, share, units. Legend lists products with share now and change vs start. Theme-aware via [Theme Mode]. Font Outfit.
