Onboarding runway (Rocketlane, PSA / customer onboarding). Seven project phases (Kickoff, Discovery, Configuration, Data Migration, Testing & UAT, Go-Live, Hypercare) laid out left to right as a runway; one swimlane per customer segment (Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB). Each phase cell is a runway segment whose fill heat (blue ramp, primary #0F62FE) is the overdue rate for that segment/phase/month, with diagonal hatching + orange (#FF7832) warning edge once overdue rate >= 25%; cell height/opacity encodes task volume. Small "project" markers (dots) flow along each lane, speed slowed by overdue rate, stalled markers pile up in hot cells. Month scrubber with Play walks the last 12 months: Enterprise Configuration / Data Migration / UAT cells heat up from ~11% to ~36-40% since July 2026 (story 1). Dropdown: segment filter (All / single). Hover on every cell: segment, phase, month, overdue rate, tasks. Theme-aware via Theme Mode. IBM Plex Sans. Fits 520x460.
